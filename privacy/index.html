Local preferences

This site remembers whether you've enabled sound using your browser's local storage. It also uses session storage for the introduction and page transitions. You can mute sound with the sound button or clear this information through your browser's site-data settings.

02

Contacting me

The contact page links to my email address and public profiles. It does not submit a form or upload a message through this website. If you email me, your email provider and mine process the message, and I receive the information you choose to include.

03

Hosting and external links

Loading the site sends ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and requested URL, to the hosting provider. Linked services such as GitHub and Bluesky have their own privacy practices. The portfolio's page code does not include an analytics or advertising tracker.

04

Questions

For a question about this portfolio or its privacy notes, email lunarion31@whale.lat. This notice describes the personal portfolio only, not Spark Filter or any linked project.
