# Petal opening foley

`petal-unfold.mp3` uses recorded paper unfolding as foley for the intro's flowers.

Source: **Paper_Unfolding_Refolding.wav** by **Brokenphono**, released under CC0.
https://freesound.org/people/Brokenphono/sounds/344369/

Download: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/344/344369_6179115-hq.mp3

The excerpt is 0.90–2.20 seconds. Edits are a high-pass filter below 90 Hz, short fades, and mono MP3 conversion. Pitch and playback speed are unchanged. It loads only after sound is enabled.

## Selected garden recordings

The chime recording is CC0. It was trimmed, converted to mono MP3, level-adjusted to approximately -24 LUFS and faded at the edges. Pitch and speed are unchanged.

- `glass-chime.mp3` — 0.10–5.60 seconds from **Glass Windchime (Plain) - A MakeNoise Morphagene Reel** by **nlux**, from the forward-playing first half.
  Source and license: https://freesound.org/people/nlux/sounds/623688/
  Download: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/623/623688_9047165-hq.mp3

The paper accompanies the flower opening. The chime can ring out after the visual transition. Skipping, muting, replaying, or hiding the page stops the current sounds.
