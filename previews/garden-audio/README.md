# Garden sound studies

All source recordings are CC0. Each preview is trimmed, converted to mono MP3, level-adjusted to approximately -24 LUFS and faded at its edges. Playback pitch and speed are unchanged.

- **leaves.mp3**, 1.00–6.50 seconds from *Leaves rustle in the wind* by **Cinetony**.
  Source and license: https://freesound.org/people/Cinetony/sounds/575380/
  Download: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/575/575380_5985747-hq.mp3
- **../../assets/audio/glass-chime.mp3**, 0.10–5.60 seconds from *Glass Windchime (Plain) - A MakeNoise Morphagene Reel* by **nlux**. This excerpt is from the forward-playing first half of the reel.
  Source and license: https://freesound.org/people/nlux/sounds/623688/
  Download: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/623/623688_9047165-hq.mp3
- **water-drops.mp3**, 0.10–5.10 seconds from *Water Droplets* by **Legnalegna55**.
  Source and license: https://freesound.org/people/Legnalegna55/sounds/543649/
  Download: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/543/543649_10485775-hq.mp3
- **petal-paper.mp3**, a level-adjusted copy of the current intro's paper foley. See ../../assets/audio/README.md for its source and edits.
- **intro-pair.mp3**, a 7.7-second mix of the leaf preview fading out while the glass chime enters at 2.2 seconds. Leaf and chime gains are 0.70 and 0.55.

The listening page plays a single preview at a time, after a user presses play.

`selected-intro.mp3` uses the paper foley at 0.8 seconds, two seconds of water droplets at 2.3 seconds, and the chime at 3.55 seconds. This is the earlier water version. The recording is 9.05 seconds including the chime tail.

Both new recordings are CC0. Excerpts keep their original pitch and speed. They are trimmed, level-adjusted to approximately -24 LUFS, faded at the edges and converted to mono MP3.

The fabric combination matches the current homepage; the wood combination remains a listening-page preview.
