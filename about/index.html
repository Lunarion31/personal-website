About Jamie.
Beyond the work.

I am Jamie, a college student studying Software Engineering with a minor in AI and machine learning. I build projects that combine practical use with an individual point of view.

My starting point

Curiosity made practical.

01

Why I build

Practical work.
A personal perspective.

I value work that serves a clear purpose while still feeling made by a person.

My interest in technology started with modding. I liked taking games apart, understanding their systems, and seeing what could happen when I changed them. That early curiosity grew into a wider interest in development, design, and making things that people can enjoy.

My original site, Whale, presented a portfolio through a classic desktop layout. It included a notepad, calculator, and calendar. The project helped me develop my skills while exploring a more individual approach to web design.

02 — Working principlesCuriosity, collaboration, and care

Purpose in function.
Personality in presentation.

I aim to balance practical usefulness with a distinctive visual identity, giving equal consideration to how a project works and how people experience it.

01

Stay curious.

Start with the question that makes an idea interesting, then explore it until there is something tangible to test and improve.

02

Build together.

Some of the best work starts in conversation. Spark, for example, began as a late-night idea shared with friends and grew through collaboration.

03

Make room for people.

Whether I am working on a game-adjacent experiment or a serious product, I care about making the result understandable, enjoyable, and welcoming.

03 — Looking ahead↗

Finish the degree.
Build the company.

I am working toward completing my degree and getting my company off the ground. This portfolio is a record of that direction: the ideas I am pursuing, the people I build with, and the work I am learning from.
