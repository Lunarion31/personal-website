About meLunarion

A person behind
the pixels.

I'm Lunarion, a student with a passion for programming. I like turning ideas into things people can explore—and giving those things a little character along the way.

My starting point

Curiosity is a good place to start.

01

Why I build

The web can be useful.
It can be personal, too.

I like the parts of the internet that feel like someone made them, not just filled in a template.

My original site, Whale, turned a portfolio into a retro desktop. It had a notepad, a calculator, a calendar, and little corners to explore. Building it was a way to learn, experiment, and make a space that felt like mine.

02 — How I approach thingsA few things I care about

Make it useful.
Make it feel alive.

Good functionality and a bit of personality don't have to compete. I want the things I build to have both.
