About Jamie.
Beyond the work.

I am Jamie, a student and developer known online as Lunarion. I am interested in creating useful, well-considered digital experiences, and my projects give me the opportunity to develop that interest through practical work.

My starting point

Understanding comes before design.

01

Why I build

Practical work.
A personal perspective.

I value work that serves a clear purpose while reflecting the care and judgment of the person behind it.

My original site, Whale, presented a portfolio through a classic desktop layout. It included a notepad, calculator, and calendar. The project helped me develop my skills while exploring a more individual approach to web design.

02 — Working principlesClarity, learning, and care

Purpose in function.
Care in presentation.

I aim to balance practical usefulness with a distinctive visual identity, giving equal consideration to how a project works and how people experience it.

01

Stay curious.

02

Keep learning.

Use each project to develop new skills, assess decisions, and identify opportunities for improvement.

03

Notice the details.

Consider the smaller decisions that shape the overall experience, from the clarity of a page to the consistency of its controls.
