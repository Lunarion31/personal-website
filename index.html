03 — My approachPrinciples that guide my work

Thoughtful work.
Lasting attention.

I approach each project as an opportunity to solve a practical problem, develop my understanding, and improve the experience for the people using it.

01

Clarity and character.

A clear structure helps people find what they need. Distinctive visual details give each project its own identity without compromising usability.

02

Learning through practice.

I develop an initial idea, assess the result, and refine it. Each project offers an opportunity to strengthen both my judgment and my skills.

03

Attention to detail.

I consider how each part of a website contributes to the overall experience, including navigation, movement, and the ability to choose whether sound is enabled.

05 — Frequently asked questionsAdditional information

information.

Answers to common questions about my work and this portfolio.

Who is Jamie, and why Lunarion?

My name is Jamie. Lunarion is the name I use online and for my public projects. I am a student and developer interested in thoughtful design, practical problem-solving, and continued learning.

What is Whale?

Whale is a personal website inspired by the classic Windows desktop. It includes a notepad, calculator, calendar, and movable windows. The project code is available on GitHub.

What happened to the old desktop?

This portfolio introduces a new visual direction while retaining the personal character of the original site. The earlier desktop project remains available in the Whale repository.

Does this site make sound?

Sound is optional and can be enabled or muted using the sound control. Your preference is saved in this browser. On a new visit, the browser may require you to interact with the page before sound can play.

Can I get in touch?

For project inquiries or potential collaboration, please email lunarion31@whale.lat. You can also contact me on Discord as @lunarion31.
