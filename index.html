03 — My approachPrinciples that guide my work

A few things
I come back to.

I work best with room to explore, people I trust, and an idea that gives us something to care about.

01

Clarity and character.

A clear structure helps people find what they need. Distinctive visual details give each project its own identity without compromising usability.

02

Learning through practice.

I prefer hands-on, exploratory work to rigid processes. Building, testing, and refining ideas is how I develop both my judgment and my skills.

03

Good work is collaborative.

I am laid-back, easygoing, and happiest when a project gives people room to share ideas, solve problems together, and enjoy the process.

05 — Frequently asked questionsAdditional information

A few
questions.

A little more context, if you were wondering.

Who is Jamie?

I am a Software Engineering student with a minor in AI and machine learning, interested in thoughtful design, practical problem-solving, and continued learning.

What is Whale?

Whale is a personal website inspired by the classic Windows desktop. It includes a notepad, calculator, calendar, and movable windows. The project code is available on GitHub.

What happened to the old desktop?

This portfolio introduces a new visual direction while retaining the personal character of the original site. The earlier desktop project remains available in the Whale repository.

Does this site make sound?

Sound is optional. The sound control manages little interaction sounds, while Play lo-fi starts a separate background music loop. Music is off in a fresh tab, and its volume and playback position are remembered for that tab. It pauses when you leave the tab. Your browser may ask for another tap to resume music after changing pages.

Can I get in touch?

For project inquiries or potential collaboration, please email lunarion31@whale.lat. You can also contact me on Discord as @lunarion31.

What am I working toward?

I am working toward completing my degree and building a company of my own. In the meantime, I am continuing to develop projects, learn from the people around me, and look for ideas worth pursuing.
