05 — A few good questionsIn case you're wondering

A little
more context.

The short version of a few things you might want to know.

Who is Lunarion?

I'm a student and developer who likes making things for the web. This is my personal portfolio—a home for my projects, experiments, and the occasional small detail.

What is Whale?

Whale is my retro desktop website. It brings a Windows-inspired interface into the browser, with a notepad, calculator, calendar, live clock, and movable windows. Its source is on GitHub.

What happened to the old desktop?

This portfolio takes a different visual direction, but keeps the same idea: a personal website should feel personal. The original desktop project lives on in the Whale repository.

Does this site make sound?

Only if you turn it on. The sound button lets you enable or mute the little UI sounds. Your preference is remembered in this browser; browser autoplay rules may still require a click on a new visit.

Can I get in touch?

Absolutely. Email lunarion31@whale.lat or find me on Discord as @lunarion31. Project questions, interesting ideas, and a simple hello are all welcome.
